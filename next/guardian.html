How it’s being built to work.

  1. Known callers

    Family rings straight through.

    The numbers Mom knows, like the kids, the grandkids and the doctor’s office, will reach her with no extra step.

  2. Unknown callers

    Strangers meet VERA first.

    She’ll answer politely and ask who’s calling and why, so Mom doesn’t have to.

  3. During a call

    Pressure gets named.

    If a caller pushes gift cards, wire transfers, secrecy or urgency, VERA will say so out loud and send you a text.

  4. Every Sunday

    A note for you.

    A short note about how Mom sounded, how many calls came in, and anything that got blocked.

One text a week,in plain words.

Nothing to log in to. Just a note from VERA, the way a thoughtful neighbor would tell you.
